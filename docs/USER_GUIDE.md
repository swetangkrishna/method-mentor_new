# Method Mentor — user guide

Updated 7 November 2026. Website: https://swetang-krishna-method-mentor-students.hf.space/

Method Mentor’s tutor, Aoife, helps you explain and compare research methods. You make the research decisions and discuss them with your supervisor. The current website runs rehearsal v12: use fictional or sample research information, without personal, confidential or identifiable participant details.

## Start

1. Sign in using the account supplied by the researcher.
2. Under **Before the conversation**, select **Read study information**. Read the information about recording and storage.
3. If you agree, tick the consent box and select **Save consent**. Wait for confirmation that consent was saved.
4. Leave **Conversation topic** on **My own research project**, then select **Start conversation**.
5. Describe your question and answer Aoife’s questions. Select **Send reply** once and wait for the response.

Aoife asks about your programme, methods experience, purpose, evidence and practical constraints. You can say that you are unsure, ask for clarification, or explain why a suggested method is impractical. These are useful parts of the discussion. The tutor can make mistakes; its reply is not approval of your research design.

## Save, leave and return

Accepted messages are saved by the server. Under **Keep or resume this conversation**, copy your **Session code** somewhere private. You can also select **Prepare my transcript** and copy the text shown under **Your transcript**.

After returning or refreshing, select **Find my conversations**, choose the conversation, and select **Open selected conversation**. Alternatively, enter the session code under **Keep or resume this conversation** and select **Resume using this code**. Sign in with the same account; another account cannot open your conversation.

A conversation from an earlier rehearsal version may open as **read-only**. Its transcript remains available. A new version may require you to read the information and save consent again before starting a new rehearsal.

## Finish and give feedback

When you want to stop, select **End conversation**. Tick **I can now explain my choice of research method** only if that describes your own position.

Open **Feedback**, answer all five rating questions and add an optional comment. Select **Save feedback** and wait for a saved confirmation. The **After the conversation** section contains a final reasoning assessment, confidence rating and ten usability questions. If the researcher asks you to complete it, answer in your own words, submit every question and wait for confirmation.

Feedback and the final assessment are separate saves. Ending a conversation alone does not submit either of them.

## If something goes wrong

- **A safety-filter message:** keep the reference number and tell the researcher, along with the session code, approximate time and what you were trying to discuss. A block does not establish that your research topic is inappropriate. Repeated sends may produce the same block.
- **A generation or connection error:** copy your unsent reply to a private note. Reopen the saved conversation and check its latest message before sending again; this helps avoid duplicate submissions after a connection interruption.
- **Red “Error” panels or a misleading “Ready” status:** an older open page can retain these panels. V12 shows reply failures in the session status field and preserves the pending input. Copy your unsent reply before refreshing, then use the recovery steps above.
- **The tutor repeatedly asks for a summary:** tell the researcher. This was reproduced during testing; v12 adds a correction. Report any recurrence. You can end the conversation without resolving every question.
- **Sign-in required:** sign in again with your existing account, then reopen your saved conversation.

Do not start another conversation merely to recover an existing one. A failed reply may remain in the reply box without being part of the saved transcript.

## Withdrawal and privacy

The **Withdraw from this study** section explains deletion. Confirming withdrawal deletes active records for that study version and prevents further participation in that version. This cannot be undone through the app. Contact the researcher about backups, retention, or records in other versions. Signing out or ending a conversation does not withdraw participation.

Conversation text, consent and research events are stored in Azure SQL. The Azure model service processes the conversation to generate replies. Follow the participant information for the study’s full terms and researcher contact details. Keep your password, session code and copied transcript private.
